Title: Constructed Wetlands
Category: Water Treatment / Flood Buffering
Problem Solved: Treating wastewater or stormwater while creating habitat and flood storage.
Historical Origin: Communities have long used wetlands and marshes as natural filters. Constructed wetlands are a modern engineered revival of this ecological principle.
How It Worked: Water moves through planted wetland cells where sediments settle and biological processes reduce pollutants.
Evidence It Worked: Nature-based solutions guidance recognizes wetlands as part of urban resilience and water treatment portfolios when properly designed and maintained.
Why It Declined or Was Overlooked: Cities often drained wetlands for development and replaced ecological treatment with centralized concrete systems.
Modern Revival Potential: Can serve schools, peri-urban settlements, parks, and decentralized wastewater projects.
Risks: Needs land, hydraulic design, mosquito management, and regular maintenance.
Verdict: Worth Reviving where land and governance permit.
Source Notes: World Bank nature-based solutions guidance and wastewater treatment literature.
